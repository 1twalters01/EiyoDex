# Executive Summary
## 1. Problem Statement
Many modern apps are shifting towards closed-source, subscription service based models, that limit transparency, user control and customisation.
They often introduce bugs, security issues, poor performance and scalability issues via "vibe coding".
As a consequence, (or as a feature), community support is constrained, plugins are restricted, and user privacy is compromised through data collection.
This is especially rife in the nutrition field, where sensitive user data a function of the space.

## 2. Solution
Eiyodex is an offline, ad free, telemetry free nutrition tracker focused on precision, reliability and ease of use.
It gives users control over their data and their experience - from the nutrients tracked to the units used in tracking.
This app intends to support plugins to allow users to add their own functionality.


# Business Goals
## 1. Product Goals
    1. To deliver offline, ad free nutrition tracking
    2. To enable users to have full data ownership
    3. To become the most trusted nutrition app in the market

## 2. Technical Goals
    1. To build a robust, extensible plugin architecture
    2. To have first in class speed, reliability and accuracy
    3. To ensure long-term maintainability

## 3. Monetization Goals
    1. To have free desktop applications to draw in customers
    2. To have one-time purchase mobile apps to support development
    3. To have non-invasive donation options

## 4. Market Goals
    1. To differentiate on transparency, extensibility and privacy
    2. To capture:
        * privacy-focused users
        * developers
        * clinicians
        * those wanting to customise their nutrient targets/foods

## 5. Long-Term Goals
    1. To create an ecosystem of modules centered around health
